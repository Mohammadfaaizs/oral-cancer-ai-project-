# Obtain the real MeMoSA data

The actual MeMoSA images are **not bundled** with this project. The official 2026 Scientific Data paper says the 30,039 images and CSV metadata are hosted on the MeMoSA Workbench and that access requires account registration, approval, a restricted-data request, agreement to applicable Data Use Agreements, and approval.

Official Workbench: https://workbench.memosa.my
Official paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-06998-7

Do not use a scraped mirror or an unverified copy. Put only the data you are authorized to use on your local machine.
